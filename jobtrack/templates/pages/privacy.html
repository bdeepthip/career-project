Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ last_updated }}

GAJ (Get A Job) helps you keep track of your job applications. This page explains, in plain language, what we store, why, and what control you have over it.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email address, and your password as a salted hash (we never see or store the password itself). If you add one, your mobile number, used only to text you log-in codes.
  • What you enter: job applications, notes, your profile (headline, phone, location, about), skills, education and experience.
  • Emails you send to GAJ: emails you paste into the Inbox or forward to your GAJ address: sender, subject and text. Attachments are not stored.
  • Your resume: if you upload one, the PDF file, kept so Easy Apply can send it.
  • Easy Apply applications: when you apply to a job posted on GAJ, a copy of your profile (name, email, phone if added, headline, location, skills, education, experience), your resume and your note, plus the status the employer gives it.
  • Employer accounts: your company name and the jobs you post.
  • Messages: messages you send to and receive from other GAJ users, plus anyone you block or report. Only you and the person you're talking to can read a conversation; if you report someone, we review the reported conversation.
  • Time on GAJ: how many minutes a day you actively use GAJ, to show your progress toward your daily goal.
  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs (IP address, pages requested, time), kept briefly to keep the service running and to block abuse such as password guessing.

Your email account

GAJ never signs in to your Gmail or Outlook account and cannot read your mailbox. It only receives the emails you choose to forward to your GAJ address (for example, with a Gmail filter). You can stop at any time by removing the filter or forwarding address in your email settings, or by getting a new GAJ address on the Inbox page.

How we use it

Only to run GAJ for you: showing your applications, working out Match Scores, and updating application statuses from emails. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or recruiters, or use it for advertising. The only time an employer sees your information is when you choose to Easy Apply to their job.

What employers see

Employers on GAJ see nothing about you unless you Easy Apply to one of their jobs. When you do, that employer (and only that employer) receives the copy described above. They can download your resume and message you on GAJ. If you withdraw the application or delete your account, it's removed from their view. GAJ doesn't share or sell your profile to employers, recruiters or anyone else.

Who else handles it

Like most websites, GAJ runs on outside providers: a hosting company that runs the app, a database provider that stores your data, an email service that sends password reset emails, an email service that receives forwarded emails, and, if you log in with your phone, a text-message service that sends your codes. They process data only to provide those services to GAJ. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your choices

  • Download everything: Account → Download my data gives you a JSON file of everything GAJ stores about you.
  • Delete everything: Account → Delete my account permanently removes your account and all your data, including your conversations with others. It may remain in encrypted backups for a short time until they are replaced.
  • Correct it: you can edit or delete any application, profile entry or email at any time.

Beta

GAJ is in beta and run by an individual developer. We take reasonable measures to protect your data (hashed passwords, encrypted connections, access limited to your own account), but no online service can promise perfect security. Please don't store information you would not want to risk, such as government ID numbers or bank details.

Age

GAJ is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell account holders by email.
